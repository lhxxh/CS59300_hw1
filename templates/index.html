Connect your AI

Search, save, and upload work right away. To summarize papers and ask questions, connect a model on the server.

  1. Copy .env.example to .env in the project folder.
  2. Set LLM_API_STYLE, LLM_API_KEY, LLM_BASE_URL, and LLM_MODEL. For Azure, use your deployment name.
  3. Restart the server, then refresh the connection below.

Your API key stays in the server configuration. The README includes examples for Azure Responses, OpenAI, DeepSeek, and Ollama.

A small guide to Folio

  1. Discover. Search real papers from arXiv or Crossref by topic or title.
  2. Keep what matters. Save a result to your library, or upload your own PDF. Your papers and conversations survive restarts.
  3. Read with a companion. Select a saved paper, generate a summary, or ask a question. arXiv PDFs are fetched when needed; attach a PDF for other sources.
  4. Check the evidence. Click page citations to read the source. The AI uses extracted paper text; verify its answers against the original.

The interface, summaries, and AI responses use English.
